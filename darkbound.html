Dark-world VR — ALPHA 0.1
VR (Quest browser, HTTPS): press ENTER VR.
• Slap your hands on the ground / walls and push to move
• Push down hard and fling your arms to jump
• Right stick: snap turn  •  B / Y: respawn
• Voice chat turns on when you enter VR  •  X: mute
• Dark Cam: click a thumbstick to hold it in that hand (or grab it with grip), trigger = photo, A = next camera mode
• Dark Cam viewer: open this game on a PC with ?cam on the end of the link (same room) to watch and record your Dark Cam live
• Battles (new!): touch a roaming monster. Touch / 1-4: pick, swing / Space: slap, hand / WASD: dodge
• Hat Stand: by spawn, buy hats with bananas
• Story mode: jump into the Rift (the light fountain at spawn) to meet Claude. Chapters 1, 2 and 2.5
• Bosses: the arena is south-east. Touch a statue to summon, dodge, then slap the boss when it's dizzy
Desktop preview: click to look, WASD move, Space jump, Shift run, R respawn, C camera (first / behind / front), M mic on / mute, V camera view, G camera mode (shoulder / selfie / orbit / boss), P photo, E or click: summon a boss at a statue / hit the boss
