Dark-world VR prototype — movement + map
VR (Quest browser, HTTPS): press ENTER VR.
• Slap your hands on the ground / walls and push to move
• Push down hard and fling your arms to jump
• Right stick: snap turn  •  B / Y: respawn
• Voice chat turns on when you enter VR  •  X: mute
• Camera: grab it with grip, trigger = photo, A = back to your shoulder
Desktop preview: click to look, WASD move, Space jump, Shift run, R respawn, C camera (first / behind / front), M mic on / mute, V camera view, G camera to shoulder, P photo
